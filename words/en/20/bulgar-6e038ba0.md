# Bulgar

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of a migratory Turkic people from Central Asia who conquered Moesia in the 7th century and settled what is now Bulgaria, and some of whom then migrated to the Volga basin to establish Volga B
