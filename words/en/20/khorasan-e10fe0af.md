# Khorasan

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Sassanian and post-Islamic Persian name for the region of Parthia located in north east of Persia (Iran), sometimes covering also parts of Central Asia and Afghanistan; the Greater Khorasan.
