# Dönmeh

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A group of crypto-Jews in the Ottoman Empire who, to avoid imprisonment after declaring arrival of the doomsday messiah, converted publicly to Islam, but retained their Jewish beliefs in secret.
