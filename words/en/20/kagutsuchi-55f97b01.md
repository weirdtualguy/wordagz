# Kagutsuchi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Japanese god of fire, son of Izanagi and Izanami and brother of Amaterasu, Susanoo, Tsukuyomi and Ōyamatsumi; he burned his mother to death while being born.
