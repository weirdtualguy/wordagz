# cockatiel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Nymphicus hollandicus, a small, rather atypical cockatoo with a distinctive pointed yellow crest, native to Australia. Avicultural forms come in many color mutations such as White Face Gray, White Fac
