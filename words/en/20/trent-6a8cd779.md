# Trent

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A river in central England, flowing 298 km (185 mi.) from Staffordshire, through Derbyshire, Leicestershire, Nottinghamshire and Lincolnshire, to join the Ouse at Trent Falls and form the Humber estua
