# Chitral

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A princely state in alliance with British India until 1947, then a princely state of Pakistan until 1969. The area of the state now forms the Chitral District of the Malakand Division, Khyber-Pakhtunk
