# syconium

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A collective fleshy fruit, in which the ovaries are hidden within a hollow receptacle, as in the fig; a hollow ball with a stalk at one end and an opening (ostiole) at the other, with flowers or fruit
