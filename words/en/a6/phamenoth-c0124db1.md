# Phamenoth

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The seventh month of the later ancient Egyptian civil calendar and Coptic calendar, corresponding to the third month of the season of Peret. Since 25 BCE, when the calendar was reformed to include lea
