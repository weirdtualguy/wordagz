# pennywort

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any member of the species Umbilicus rupestris of succulent, perennial flowering plants (penny-pies, wall pennywort, kidney wort, navelwort) in the stonecrop family (Crassulaceae).
