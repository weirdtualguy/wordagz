# Arsacid

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of or pertaining to the Parthian dynasty established by Arsaces I which ruled the Parthian Empire during 247 BC – 224 AD, Armenia during 54–428, as well as Iberia and Caucasian Albania
