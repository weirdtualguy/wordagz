# tensor veli palatini

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A ribbonlike muscle of the palate that arises from the scaphoid fossa, from the spine of the sphenoid bone, and from the lateral cartilaginous wall of the Eustachian tube, that is attached by a tendon
