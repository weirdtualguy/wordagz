# Kirchnerism

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The political philosophy of Néstor Kirchner, president of Argentina from 2003 to 2007, and his wife Cristina Fernández de Kirchner, president from 2007 to 2015.
