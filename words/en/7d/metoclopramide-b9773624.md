# metoclopramide

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A procainamide derivative that is a dopamine D₂-receptor antagonist, given (usually as the hydrochloride) orally or by injection as an antiemetic and stimulant of gastrointestinal motility; 4-amino-5-
