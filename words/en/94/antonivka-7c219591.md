# Antonivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former village in Buky silrada, Buky Raion, Cherkasy Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, first mentioned in 1781, annexed to the raion’s administrative centre, Buky, in July 1958.
