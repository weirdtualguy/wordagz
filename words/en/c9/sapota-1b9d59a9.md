# sapota

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The tropical fruit from the sapodilla tree, Manilkara zapota. The fruit is 4–8 cm in diameter, has a fuzzy brown skin with very sweet earthy-brown flesh.
