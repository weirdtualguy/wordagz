# myopia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A disorder of the vision where distant objects appear blurred because the eye focuses their images in front of the retina instead of on it.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [μυωπία](../../grc/2f/w-57e4128f.md) ("shortsightedness").
