# Sumida

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A special ward of Tokyo prefecture, Japan that is surrounded by (clockwise from north) Adachi, Katsushika, Edogawa, Kōtō, Chūō, Taitō, and Arakawa special wards.
