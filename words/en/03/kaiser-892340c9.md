# Kaiser

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An emperor of a German-speaking country, particularly the Holy Roman Empire (962–1806), the Austrian Empire/Austria-Hungary (1806–1918), or the German Empire (1871–1918) — often specifically Wilhelm I
