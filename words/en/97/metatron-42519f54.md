# Metatron

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An archangel and the chancellor of Heaven and the scribe of the book of life, in Christian and Jewish folklore. According to medieval apocrypha, he is Enoch, ancestor of Noah, transformed into an ange
