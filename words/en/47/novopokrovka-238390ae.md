# Novopokrovka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former selrada of Dnipropetrovsk Raion, Dnipropetrovsk Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Novopokrovka settlement hromada in August 2015.
