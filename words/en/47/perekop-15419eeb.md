# Perekop

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former fortress-city on the Crimea, founded in antiquity as Taphros, believed to have first been fortified by the Crimean Scythians, rendered uninhabitable in November 1920 by the Perekop–Chonhar Op
