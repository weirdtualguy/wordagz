# arthrostracous

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Pertaining to the Arthrostraca, an obsolete grouping of crustaceans with segmented thoraxes and abdomens, bearing seven pairs of thoracic legs; included in this group are the isopods and amphipods.
