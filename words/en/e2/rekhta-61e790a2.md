# Rekhta

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A style of Hindi-Urdu, Hindavi, or sometimes Braj or Punjabi used in poetry that linguistically adapts Persian grammar and vocabulary (to a large extent); a mix of Persian and Khariboli.
