# Boikivske

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former selrada (until 1971, a silrada) of Boikivske Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1923, merged into Boikivske settlement hromada in June 2020.
