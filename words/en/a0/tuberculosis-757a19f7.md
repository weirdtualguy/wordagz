# tuberculosis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An infectious disease of humans and animals caused by a species of mycobacterium, usually Mycobacterium tuberculosis, mainly infecting the lungs where it causes tubercles characterized by the expector
