# Betelgeuse

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A bright-red supergiant intrinsic variable star, the second brightest star in the constellation Orion; Alpha (α) Orionis. It is the tenth brightest star in the nighttime, and one of the largest stars
