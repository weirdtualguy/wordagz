# Zipser

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of the Germanic-speaking ethnic group which developed in the Zips region of Upper Hungary (now Slovakia) beginning in the 13th century, and subsequently settled also in northern Romani, Bukov
