# Nishnaabemwin

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The variants of Nishnaabemwin that use this form of the word (which are spoken with vowel syncope), spoken in southern Ontario, around Lake Huron, primarily Odawa, Eastern Ojibwe and Nipissing.
