# Petun

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of the Iroquoian "tobacco people", or Tionontati, a First Nation located on the southwest edge of the Georgian Bay of Lake Huron, west of the Huron territory in Southern Ontario.
