# Ennead

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The collection of nine gods worshipped at Heliopolis and taking part in the Heliopolitan creation myth, together representing the sum of all the elements of the created world: Atum, Shu, Tefnut, Nut,
