# acrophony

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The naming of letters in an alphabetic writing system using words whose initial sounds are represented by the respective letters.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [ἄκρος](../../grc/22/w-8215416b.md) ("highest, on the extremity").
