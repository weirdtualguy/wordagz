# chisel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A cutting tool used to remove parts of stone, wood or metal by pushing or pounding the back when the sharp edge is against the material. It consists of a slim, oblong block of metal with a sharp wedge
