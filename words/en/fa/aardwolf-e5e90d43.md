# aardwolf

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A nocturnal, insectivorous mammal (Proteles cristatus), having yellowish fur with vertical black stripes, and a long mane down the midline of the neck and back; it is related to and resembles the hyen
