# Bastet

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The goddess of the sun and the moon, of cats, and Lower Egypt; one of the "Eyes / Daughters of Ra", the consort of Ptah, often depicted as having a she-cat's head.
