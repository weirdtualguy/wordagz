# flamboyer

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of various trees in the East and West Indies with brilliant blossoms, probably species of Caesalpinieae, especially of Delonix and Caesalpinia, all of which were formerly in the obsolete genus Poi
