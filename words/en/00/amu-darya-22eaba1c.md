# Amu Darya

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A major river in Central Asia, flowing along the borders of Afghanistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan, emptying into the (now mostly dry) Aral Sea.
