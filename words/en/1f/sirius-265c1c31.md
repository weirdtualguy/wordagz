# Sirius

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A telescopic binary star, visually the brightest star in the night sky, a part of the northern constellation of Canis Major (the Greater Dog), one of three stars in the Winter Triangle asterism. Long
