# heterodox

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of or pertaining to creeds, beliefs, or teachings, especially religious ones, that are different from what a religion (or sect) believes to be orthodox, but (usually) not conflicting with that religio
