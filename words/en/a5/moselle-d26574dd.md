# Moselle

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A left tributary of Rhine, flowing through the departments of Vosges, Meurthe-et-Moselle and Moselle in northeastern France, through Luxembourg, and through the states of Rhineland-Palatinate and Saar
