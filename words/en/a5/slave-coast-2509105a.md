# Slave Coast

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A region of West Africa on the coast of the Gulf of Guinea in the Atlantic Ocean from the Volta River to the Lagos Lagoon or Niger Delta, now comprising the coasts of Togo, Benin, and parts of Nigeria
