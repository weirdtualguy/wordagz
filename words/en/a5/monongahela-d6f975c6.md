# Monongahela

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An Iroquoian Native American culture from AD 1050 to 1635 in present-day western Pennsylvania, western Maryland, eastern Ohio, and West Virginia, whose sites have been mostly found in the Monongahela
