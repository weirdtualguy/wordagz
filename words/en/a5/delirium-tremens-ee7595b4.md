# delirium tremens

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A severe, potentially life-threatening form of alcohol withdrawal characterized by the abrupt onset of global confusion, autonomic hyperactivity, and profound neuropsychiatric disturbances.
