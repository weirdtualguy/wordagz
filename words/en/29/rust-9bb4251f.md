# rust

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The deteriorated state of iron or steel as a result of moisture and oxidation; it consists mostly of iron(III) oxide (ferric oxide) and iron(II) oxide (ferrous oxide).
