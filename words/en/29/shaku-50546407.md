# shaku

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The Japanese foot, a traditional Japanese unit of length equal to 10 sun or ¹/₁₀ of a jō, now standardized as equal to ¹⁰/₃₃ of a meter.
