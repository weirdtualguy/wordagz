# Balt

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An ethnic descendant of the Indo-European Baltic people, especially including ethnic Lithuanians, Latvians or Prussians, but generally not including ethnic Belarusians, Estonians, Germans, Jews, Livon
