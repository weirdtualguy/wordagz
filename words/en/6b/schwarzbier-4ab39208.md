# schwarzbier

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A dark lager that has an opaque, black colour with hints of chocolate or coffee flavours, and is generally around 5% ABV, similar to stout in that it is made from roasted malt, which gives it its dark
