# dolmadaki

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A rice mixture with fresh herbs such as mint and parsley and, sometimes, pine nuts, with tender grape leaves, served with a thick and creamy, lemony sauce. Also, in some regions, minced meat is added
