# metzitza

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

a procedure performed in Rabbinic Judaism after the Jewish ritual of brit milah (covenent cut) following the two added steps of priya (uncovering) and circumcision (circle-cut) mandated by the Gelmud
