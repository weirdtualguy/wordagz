# Aonia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient region of Greece: possibly Boeotia, or possibly the part of Boeotia containing the mountains Helicon and Cithaeron, and thus sacred to the Muses, whom Ovid calls the Aonides.
