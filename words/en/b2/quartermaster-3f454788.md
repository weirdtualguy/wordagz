# quartermaster

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An officer whose duty is to provide quarters, provisions, storage, clothing, fuel, stationery, and transportation for a regiment or other body of troops, and superintend the supplies. Master of quarte
