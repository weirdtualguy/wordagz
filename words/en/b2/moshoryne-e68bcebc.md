# Moshoryne

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A village in Subottsi rural hromada, Kropyvnytskyi Raion, Kirovohrad Oblast, Ukraine, resettled and renamed by migrants from the Pannonian Plain in 1752.
