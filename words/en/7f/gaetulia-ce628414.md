# Gaetulia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient district covering the large desert region south of the Atlas Mountains, bordering the Sahara. Other documents place it in pre-Roman times along the Mediterranean coasts of what is now Alger
