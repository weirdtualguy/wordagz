# peroneus longus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A muscle of the lateral side of the leg which arises from the head and side of the fibula, ends in a long tendon that inserts on to the cuneiform and first metatarsal bones on the medial side via a lo
