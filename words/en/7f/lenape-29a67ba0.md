# Lenape

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of a group of aboriginal Americans who were living in what is now New Jersey and along the Delaware River in Pennsylvania, the coast of Delaware, and the lower Hudson Valley and New York Harb
