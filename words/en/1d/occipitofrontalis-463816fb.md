# occipitofrontalis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A large flat muscle on each side of the vertex of the skull, consisting of an occipital portion (arising from the occipital and temporal bones) and a frontal portion that are separated by the epicrani
