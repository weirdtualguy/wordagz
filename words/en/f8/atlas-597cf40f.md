# Atlas

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The son of Iapetus and Clymene, war leader of the Titans ordered by the god Zeus to support the sky on his shoulders; father to the Hesperides, the Hyades, and the Pleiades; king of the legendary Atla
