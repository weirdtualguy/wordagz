# chorda tympani

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A branch of the facial nerve that traverses the middle ear cavity and the infratemporal fossa and supplies autonomic fibers to the sublingual and submandibular glands and sensory fibers to the anterio
