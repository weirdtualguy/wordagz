# dock

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of the genus Rumex of coarse weedy plants with small green flowers related to buckwheat, especially bitter dock (Rumex obtusifolius), and used as potherbs and in folk medicine, especially in curin
