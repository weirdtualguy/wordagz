# Vyrnwy

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A river in Powys, Wales, and Shropshire, England, flowing about 40 miles from Lake Vyrnwy in Powys before joining the Severn near Melverley in Shropshire.
