# marmalade

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A kind of jam made with citrus fruit, distinguished by being made slightly bitter by the addition of the peel and by partial caramelisation during manufacture. Most commonly made with Seville oranges,
