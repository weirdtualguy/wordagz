# Pegasus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A winged horse fabled to have sprung from the blood of Medusa when she was slain. He is noted for causing, with a blow of his hoof, Hippocrene, the inspiring fountain of the Muses, to spring from Moun
