# negotiorum gestio

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A quasi-contract imposing a duty to reimburse an intervenor (gestor) who has seen to, managed, or looked after the affairs, property, or interests of another (dominus), but without the latter’s consen
