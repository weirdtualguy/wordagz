# fynbos

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Vegetation unique to the Cape Floral Kingdom, largely scrub, exceptionally biodiverse, including particularly large numbers of species of Proteaceae, Restionaceae, and Ericaceae.
