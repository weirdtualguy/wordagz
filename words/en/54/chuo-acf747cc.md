# Chūō

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A special ward of Tokyo prefecture, Japan that is surrounded by (clockwise from north) Taitō, Sumida, Kōtō, Minato and Chiyoda special wards as well as Tokyo Bay.
