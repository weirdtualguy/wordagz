# yawn

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** verb

## Definition

To open the mouth widely and take a long, rather deep breath, often because one is tired or bored, and sometimes accompanied by pandiculation.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [gānian](../../ang/d2/ganian-10c967e6.md) ("to yawn, gape").
