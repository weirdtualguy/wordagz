# Ländchen

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The German name given to several Ice Age plateaux in the Havelland region, which climb to over 70 meters above the formerly marshy urstromtal levels (old glacial meltwater valleys) of the Havelland Lu
