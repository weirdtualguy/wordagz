# Most Serene Republic of Venice

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Synonym of Venice, a former polity in Europe, a republic and colonial empire in the Adriatic and eastern Mediterranean from AD 697 to 1797 with its capital at Venice.
