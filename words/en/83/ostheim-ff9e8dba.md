# Ostheim

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Former name of Boikivske selrada: a former selrada (until 1971, a silrada) of Boikivske Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name until February 1935.
