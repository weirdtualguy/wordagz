# Actistetes

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

a Julianist sect which stood in opposition to the Ctistolatræ an believed that Jesus shouldn't be spoken of as a created being, which was a form of Docetism and anti-Nicenean.
