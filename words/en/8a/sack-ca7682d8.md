# sack

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A bag; especially a large bag of strong, coarse material for storage and handling of various commodities, such as potatoes, coal, coffee; or, a bag with handles used at a supermarket, a grocery sack;
