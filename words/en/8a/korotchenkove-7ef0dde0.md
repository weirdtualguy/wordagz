# Korotchenkove

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Former name of Pohrebky: a village, the administrative centre of Pohrebky starostynskyi okruh, Shostka urban hromada, Shostka Raion, Sumy Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name 1969–2016.
