# camphor

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A white transparent waxy crystalline isoprenoid ketone, 1,7,7-trimethylbicyclo\[2.2.1\]heptan-2-one, with a strong pungent odour, used in pharmacy, also known as laurel camphor or common/ordinary campho
