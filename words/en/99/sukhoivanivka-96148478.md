# Sukhoivanivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Former name of Stepnohirsk: a rural settlement, the administrative centre of Stepnohirsk settlement hromada, Vasylivka Raion, Zaporizhzhia Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name until 1987.
