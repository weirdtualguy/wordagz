# palatopharyngeus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A long, fleshy longitudinal muscle, narrower in the middle than at either end, that arises from the soft palate, contributes with the mucous membrane covering its surface to the structure of the palat
