# avagraha

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A symbol (ऽ) used in the Devanagari script and other Indian scripts, to mark vocalic gemination or the elision of the inherent vowel a in the process of sandhi in Sanskrit.
