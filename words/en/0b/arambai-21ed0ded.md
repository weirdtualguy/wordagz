# arambai

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Meitei traditional dart-like weapon, poisoned at its sharp tip, having peacock-feathered ends, to ease its flight when thrown or projected towards the enemies, frequently used by the cavalry soldier
