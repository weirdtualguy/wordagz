# dreidel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A four-sided spinning top, inscribed with the four Hebrew letters נ (nun), ג (gimel), ה (hey), and ש (shin) (or פ (pey)) on each side, often used to play a traditional game during the Jewish festival
