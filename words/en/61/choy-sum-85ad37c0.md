# choy sum

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Chinese vegetable, a variety of Brassica rapa parachinensis subspecies, similar to bok choy but slimmer, with smooth green leaves and pale green stems, and clusters of flowers on the tips of the inn
