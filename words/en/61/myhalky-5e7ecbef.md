# Myhalky

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former silrada of Borodianka Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Piskivka settlement hromada in January 2016; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement: Myhalky.
