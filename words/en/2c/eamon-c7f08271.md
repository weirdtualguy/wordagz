# Eamon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A male given name from Irish Éamonn, Éamann \[in turn from Old English Ēadmund (“prosperity protector”)\], variant of Edmund; variant forms Eamonn, Eamann, Eaman.
