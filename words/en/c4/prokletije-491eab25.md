# Prokletije

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A mountain range in the Balkans that extends from northern Albania to Kosovo, eastern Montenegro, and southernmost Serbia; it comprises the southernmost part of the Dinaric Alps.
