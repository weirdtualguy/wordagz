# blunderbuss

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An old style of muzzleloading firearm and early form of shotgun with a distinctive short, large caliber barrel that is flared at the muzzle, therefore able to fire scattered quantities of nails, stone
