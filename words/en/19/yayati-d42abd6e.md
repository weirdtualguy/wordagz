# Yayati

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The fifth king of the Lunar dynasty, son of Nahusha, the husband of Devayani and Sharmishtha, and the progenitor of the lineages of the Yadavas and the Pandavas.
