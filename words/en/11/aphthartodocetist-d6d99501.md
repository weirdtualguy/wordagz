# Aphthartodocetist

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A follower or proponent of Aphthartodocetism, the Christological doctrine that taught Christ's body was naturally incorruptible and not subject to physical suffering, associated with Julian of Halicar
