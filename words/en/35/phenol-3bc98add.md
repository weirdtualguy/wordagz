# phenol

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A caustic, poisonous, white crystalline compound, C₆H₅OH, derived from benzene and used in resins, plastics, and pharmaceuticals and in dilute form as a disinfectant and antiseptic; once called carbol
