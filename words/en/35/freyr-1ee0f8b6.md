# Freyr

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A Vanir god associated with kingship and virility in the North Germanic corpus, in which he rides the shining boar Gullinbursti and wields an antler as a weapon. He is sometimes referred to as Yngvi-F
