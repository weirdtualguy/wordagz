# manticore

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A beast with the body of a lion (usually red), the tail of a scorpion, and the head/face of a man with a mouth filled with multiple rows of sharp teeth (like a shark), said to be able to shoot spikes
