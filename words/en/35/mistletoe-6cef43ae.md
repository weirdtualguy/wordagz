# mistletoe

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of numerous hemiparasitic evergreen plants of the order Santalales with white berries that grow in the crowns of apple trees, oaks, and other trees, such as the European mistletoe (Viscum album) a
