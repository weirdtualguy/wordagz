# maleic acid

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

an unsaturated organic dicarboxylic acid, cis-butenedioic acid; HOOCCH=CHCOOH, the geometric isomer of fumaric acid; it is found in unripe fruit, and is used industrially in the production of polyeste
