# omicron

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The 15th letter of the Classical and Modern Greek alphabets (16th in Ancient and Old Greek), used in ordering lists as in naming (astronomy) the 15th star of a constellation or (epidemiology) the 15th
