# orgy

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A secret rite or ceremony, typically involving riotous and dissolute behavior, including dancing, drunkenness and indiscriminate sexual activity, undertaken in honor of various pagan gods or goddesses
