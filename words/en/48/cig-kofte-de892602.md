# cig kofte

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An Anatolian dish made from raw ground meat (often lamb or beef), bulgur, onion, tomato paste, hot red pepper paste, and urfa biber, wrapped in lettuce.
