# nete

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

In Ancient Greek musical theory, the highest-pitched fixed note in the farther tetrachord on a lyre, always pitched a perfect fourth above the paramese, with two movable notes between them, the trite
