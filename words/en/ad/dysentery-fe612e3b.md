# dysentery

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A disease characterised by inflammation of the intestines, especially the colon (large intestine), accompanied by pus (white blood cells) in the feces, fever, pain in the abdomen, high-volume diarrhea
