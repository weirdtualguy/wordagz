# Kutsurub

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former silrada of Ochakiv Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, amalgamated into Kutsurub rural hromada in October 2015; administrative centre and sole constituent settlement: Kutsurub.
