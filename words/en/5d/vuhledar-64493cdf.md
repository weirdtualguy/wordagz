# Vuhledar

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A city, the administrative centre of Vuhledar urban hromada, Volnovakha Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, founded in 1964 around the Pivdennodonbaska 1 coal mine.
