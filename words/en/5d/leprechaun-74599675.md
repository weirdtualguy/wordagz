# leprechaun

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A type of diminutive, solitary fairy or sprite, traditionally depicted as a small, bearded man wearing a green coat and hat, often found repairing a single shoe; they typically possess hidden treasure
