# soffietta

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A tool consisting of a metal tube, often swan necked, attached to a conical nozzle. The nozzle is used to block the mouth of a glass vessel so that it can be further inflated by blowing into the tube.
