# samalamig

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of the various kinds of sweet chilled Filipino beverages, such as chilled fruit juice or other flavors like chocolate, coffee, sweet corn, etc., usually mixed with shredded jelly and/or sago pearl
