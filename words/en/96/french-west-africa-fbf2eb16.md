# French West Africa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A federation of former French colonies in West Africa existing from 1895 to 1958, consisting of Dahomey (Benin), French Guinea (Guinea), the French Sudan (Mali), Ivory Coast, Mauritania, Niger, Senega
