# Antisthenes

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A transliteration of the Ancient Greek male given name Ἀντισθένης (Antisthénēs), notably borne by Antisthenes of Athens (445–365 B.C.), a Greek philosopher, founder of the school of Cynicism and a pup
