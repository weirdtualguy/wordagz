# rosemary

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A shrub, Salvia rosmarinus (formerly Rosmarinus officinalis), that originates from Europe and Asia Minor and produces a fragrant herb used in cooking and perfumes.
