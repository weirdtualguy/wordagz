# Cytherean

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of, from or relating to Cythera, an island now part of Greece, at which the goddess Aphrodite came ashore on a seashell according to Greek mythology. Thus, referring to the goddesses Aphrodite or Venu
