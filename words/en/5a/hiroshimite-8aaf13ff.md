# hiroshimite

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A rare crystalline structure for metallic alloys found in hiroshimaite glass nodules, forged by the intense temperature and pressure of an atomic bomb explosion, fusion various metals.
