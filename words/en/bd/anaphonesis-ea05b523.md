# anaphonesis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The raising of /e/, /o/ to /i/, /u/ before certain consonantal environments, mainly \[ŋ\] and /ʎ/, occurring originally in some Tuscan dialects, and thus in Standard Italian.
