# glockenspiel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A musical instrument of the percussion idiophone family of instruments. Like the xylophone, it has tuned bars arranged like the keys on a piano. It is smaller in size and higher in pitch than the xylo
