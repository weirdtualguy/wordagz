# Eurystheus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A Mycenaean king of Tiryns (or, according to some authors, of Argos), in Argolis, son of Sthenelus and grandson of Perseus, who imposed the twelve labours on Heracles.
