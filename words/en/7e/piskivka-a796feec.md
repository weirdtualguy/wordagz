# Piskivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A river, a right tributary of the Teteriv, having its course entirely within Borodianka and Piskivka settlement hromadas, Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine.
