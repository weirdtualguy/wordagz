# Maliutianka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former silrada of Kyiv-Sviatoshyn Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, established in 1972, merged into Boiarka urban hromada in June 2020.
