# massicot

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

lead monoxide, PbO, obtained as a yellow amorphous powder, the fused and crystalline form of which is called litharge; lead ocher. It is used as a pigment; also, lead oxide yellow, as opposed to red l
