# Christingle

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A small Christmas gift for children symbolizing Jesus Christ as the “light” of the world. A typical modern Christingle is made of an orange with sweets skewered into it, a candle inserted into the top
