# Vilna Gaon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Eliyahu ben Shlomo Zalman, a Jewish polymath from Vilnius renowned for his erudition and one of the most influential opponents of the developing Hasidic movement.
