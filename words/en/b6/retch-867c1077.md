# retch

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** verb

## Definition

To make or experience an unsuccessful effort to vomit; to strain or spasm, as if to vomit; to gag or nearly vomit.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [hrǣċan](../../ang/50/hrcan-65a8beab.md) ("to clear the throat, hawk, spit").
