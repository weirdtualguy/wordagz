# háček

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A caron; a diacritical mark (ˇ) usually resembling an inverted circumflex, but in the cases of ď, Ľ, ľ, and ť resembling an apostrophe (’) instead.
