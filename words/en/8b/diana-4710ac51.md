# Diana

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The daughter of Latona and Jupiter, and twin sister of Apollo; the goddess of the hunt, associated wild animals and the forest or wilderness, and an emblem of chastity; the Roman counterpart of Artemi
