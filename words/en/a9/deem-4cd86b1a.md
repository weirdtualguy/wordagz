# deem

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** verb

## Definition

To hold in belief or estimation; to adjudge as a conclusion; to regard as being; to evaluate according to one's beliefs; to account.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [dēman](../../ang/60/deman-cd0421ac.md) ("to decide, decree, deem").
