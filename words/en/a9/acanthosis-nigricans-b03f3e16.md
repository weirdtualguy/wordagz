# acanthosis nigricans

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A skin sign often associated with insulin resistance and type 2 diabetes exhibiting velvety, thickened, and darkened skin and skin tags, often occurring in skin folds and the axillae.
