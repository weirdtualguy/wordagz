# stephanion

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The point on the side of the skull where the upper temporal line, or upper edge of the temporal fossa, crosses the coronal suture.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [στέφανος](../../grc/40/w-dc7829a2.md) ("that which surrounds, crown, wreath").
