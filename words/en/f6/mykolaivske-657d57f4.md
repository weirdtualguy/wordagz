# Mykolaivske

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former village in Mykhalchyna-Sloboda silrada, Novhorod-Siverskyi Raion, Chernihiv Oblast, Ukraine, founded in the 1920s–30s, disincorporated in July 2016.
