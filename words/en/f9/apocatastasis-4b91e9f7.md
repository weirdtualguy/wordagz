# apocatastasis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An apocalypse leading to the remaking of the world rather than a Final Judgment, (Christianity) an Origenist doctrine condemned by the 543 C.E. Synod of Constantinople.
