# cui bono

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The principle that the ultimate initiator of an action is likely the person who stands to gain from the action.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [cui bonō fuisset](../../la/c3/cui-bono-fuisset-683a72f6.md) ("to whom it would have benefited").
