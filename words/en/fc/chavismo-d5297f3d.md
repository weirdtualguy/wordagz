# Chavismo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A left-wing populist ideology based on the policies of Hugo Chávez (1954–2013), the former president of Venezuela (2002–2013), and the politics of the Bolivarian Revolution (Bolivarianism).
