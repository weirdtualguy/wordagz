# Illinivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A village, the administrative centre of Illinivka rural hromada, Kramatorsk Raion, Donetsk Oblast, Ukraine, first recorded as an unnamed khutir on a map from 1888.
