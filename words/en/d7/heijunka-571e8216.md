# heijunka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The smoothing of a production process so that intermediate goods are made available at a constant rate and inventory is always available when required.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [平準化](../../ja/f3/w-a3b6aa4e.md) ("levelling").
