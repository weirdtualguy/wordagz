# Aerope

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The daughter of Catreus, king of Crete, and sister to Clymene, Apemosyne and Althaemenes. Catreus gave her to Nauplius, to be drowned or sold abroad, but Nauplius spared her.
