# kogel mogel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An egg-based homemade dessert made from egg yolks, sugar, and flavourings such as honey, vanilla, cocoa or rum, similar to eggnog, and popular in Central and Eastern Europe, as well as in the Caucasus
