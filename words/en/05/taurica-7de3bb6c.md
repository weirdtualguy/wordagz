# Taurica

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient geographic region and peninsula, the name used by the Greeks and Romans for the modern Crimean Peninsula in Eastern Europe, internationally recognized as part of Ukraine but occupied by Rus
