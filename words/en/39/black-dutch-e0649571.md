# Black Dutch

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A European with dark hair, eyes, and skin, especially one from or claiming to be from Germany or the Netherlands, sometimes (derogatory, ethnic slur) with disparaging racial overtones.
