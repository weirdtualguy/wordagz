# tacamahac

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A bitter balsamic resin or resinous exudation obtained from tropical American trees of the family Burseraceae (Bursera tomentosa and Icica tacamahaca), from East Indian trees of the genus Calophyllum,
