# Ctesiphon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient capital of Parthia and later of the Sassanid Persian Empire, on the Tigris near Baghdad in present-day Iraq, abandoned in the 7th and 8th centuries.
