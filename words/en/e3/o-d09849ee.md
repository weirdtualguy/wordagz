# -o-

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** interfix

## Definition

A linking vowel inserted interconsonantally between two morphemes, to ease pronunciation, without contributing to the meaning. It frequently joins words or combining forms of Ancient Greek or Classica
