# Peisistratid

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of or relating to the Ancient Greek tyrant Peisistratus (ruled 546–527 B.C.E.) or his family, particularly his two sons, Hipparchus and Hippias.
