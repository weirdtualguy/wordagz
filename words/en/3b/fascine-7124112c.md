# fascine

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Originally a cylindrical bundle of small sticks of wood, and now often a bundle of plastic pipes, bound together, and used for strengthening purposes, such as in revetments for riverbanks, and in mats
