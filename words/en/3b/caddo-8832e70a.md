# Caddo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of a confederacy of several southeastern Native American tribes, who inhabited much of what is now East Texas, western Louisiana and portions of southern Arkansas and Oklahoma in the 16th cen
