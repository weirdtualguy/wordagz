# Middleton

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A rural locality and civil parish (without a council) in Westmorland and Furness district, Cumbria, previously in South Lakeland district, the locality also known as Middleton in Lonsdale or Middleton
