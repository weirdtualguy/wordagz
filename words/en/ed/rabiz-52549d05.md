# rabiz

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A popular modern Armenian music style that shows Middle Eastern influences and is related to Russian blatnyak and Azerbaijani mugham. Most of the songs have a 6/8 rhythm.
