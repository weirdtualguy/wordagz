# reptile

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A (typically scaly and egg-laying) cold-blooded vertebrate tetrapod animal, of the class Reptilia (excluding birds), such as a lizard, turtle, snake, crocodile, alligator, etc.
