# Acadia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former French colony in North America in the 17th and early 18th centuries, spanning over what are now the Maritime provinces of eastern Canada (Nova Scotia, New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island)
