# Khond

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any member of an aboriginal hunter-gatherer tribe of India, inhabiting the states of Odisha and Andhra Pradesh (Srikakulam), and speakers of the Dravidian Kui and Kuvi languages.
