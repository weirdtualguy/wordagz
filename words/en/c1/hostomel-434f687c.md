# Hostomel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A rural settlement, the administrative centre of the settlement hromada of the same name of Bucha Raion, Kyiv Oblast, Ukraine, first mentioned in 1494, designated a hero city on the 6th of March 2022.
