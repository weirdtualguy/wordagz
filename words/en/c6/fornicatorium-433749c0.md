# fornicatorium

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A bedroom, or any room used for fornication; often a small podlike room within a larger fornication-related establishment; in modern times, mainly found in Japan, Hong Kong, and Taiwan.
