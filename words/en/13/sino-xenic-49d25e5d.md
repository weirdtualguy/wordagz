# Sino-Xenic

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Related to pronunciations for reading Chinese in Japan, Korea and Vietnam, originating in medieval times and the source of large-scale borrowings of Chinese words into Japonic, Koreanic and Vietnamese
