# Bollenhut

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A formal headdress with woollen pompoms, worn since about 1750 by Protestant women as part of their folk costume in the three neighbouring Black Forest villages of Gutach, Kirnbach and Hornberg-Reiche
