# Sonderbund

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Swiss cantons of Lucerne, Fribourg, Valais, Uri, Schwyz, Unterwalden, and Zug, all predominantly Catholic and governed by conservative administrations, and the subject of the Sonderbund War of 184
