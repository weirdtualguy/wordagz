# Buyan

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

In Russian folklore, a mysterious island with the ability to appear and disappear with the tide; the home of the Zoryas (solar goddesses) and three brothers representing the Northern, Western, and Eas
