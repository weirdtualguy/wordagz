# Eunomia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A minor goddess, governing law, order and lawmaking; one of the Horae, she is a daughter of Zeus and Themis, and her sisters are Eirene (Peace) and Dike (Justice); her Roman counterpart is Disciplina.
