# Alfur

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An umbrella term for indigenous people of the Moluccas having Melanesian or Austronesian ancestry and not integrated into Malay‑speaking, Islamised, or Christianised, coastal polities.
