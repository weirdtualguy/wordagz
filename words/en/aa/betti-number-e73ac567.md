# Betti number

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of a sequence of numbers, denoted bₙ, which characterise a given topological space K by giving, for each dimension, the number of holes in K of said dimension; (formally) the rank of the nth homol
