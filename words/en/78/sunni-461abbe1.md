# Sunni

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Belonging or relating to the branch of Islam based on the Qur'an, the Kutub al-Sittah (the hadiths which record the Sunnah) and that places emphasis on the Sahabah.
