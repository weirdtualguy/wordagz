# primer

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A prayer or devotional book intended for laity, initially an abridgment of the breviary and manual including the hours of the Virgin Mary, 15 gradual and 7 penitential psalms, the litany, the placebo
