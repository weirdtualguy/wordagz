# foraminifer

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of a large group of aquatic amoeboid protists of the subphylum Foraminifera, characterized by streaming granular ectoplasm that among other things is used for catching food, often with a calcareou
