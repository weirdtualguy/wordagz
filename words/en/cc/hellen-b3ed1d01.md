# Hellen

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

the mythological patriarch of the Hellenes, the son of Deucalion (or sometimes Zeus) and Pyrrha, brother of Amphictyon and father of Aeolus, Xuthus, and Dorus
