# parallax

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An apparent shift in the position of two stationary objects relative to each other as viewed by an observer, due to a change in observer position.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [παράλλαξις](../../grc/37/w-986719ab.md) ("alteration").
