# calamine

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Synonym of smithsonite, a pink form of zinc oxide (mainly zinc carbonate ZnCO₃) formed as a byproduct of zinc sublimation, now used in skin lotions.
