# zmijovka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A traditional Czech knitted wool cap with no bill, topped by a small pom-pom, and encircled with a cuff into which a pattern is knitted in which smaller zig-zags are predominated by a large central zi
