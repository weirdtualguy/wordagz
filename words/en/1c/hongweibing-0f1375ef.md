# hongweibing

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A red guard, a member of a mass movement of civilians, mostly students and other young people in China, who were mobilized by Mao Zedong in 1966 and 1967, during the Cultural Revolution.
