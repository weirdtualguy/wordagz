# mirabelle plum

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A sweet, edible drupaceous fruit of the mirabelle prune tree, a cultivar of the plum tree of the subspecies Prunus domestica subsp. syriaca, often used in pies, wine, and brandy.
