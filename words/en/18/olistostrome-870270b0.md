# olistostrome

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A sedimentary deposit composed of a chaotic mass of heterogeneous material, such as blocks and mud, known as olistoliths, that accumulates as a semifluid body by submarine gravity sliding or slumping
