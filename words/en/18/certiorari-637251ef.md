# certiorari

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A grant of the right of an appeal to be heard by an appellate court where that court has discretion to choose which appeals it will hear.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [certiōrō](../../la/2f/certioro-bb1ad9f6.md) ("to make certain").
