# Edogawa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A special ward of Tokyo prefecture, Japan that is surrounded by (clockwise from north) Katsushika special ward, Chiba Prefecture, Kōtō, and Sumida special wards.
