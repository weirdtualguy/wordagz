# Donghak

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An academic movement in Korean Neo-Confucianism, founded in 1860 by Choe Je-u, that arose as a reaction to seohak (西學, "Western learning") and gradually evolved into the religion known as Cheondoism.
