# frikadelle

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An oblate-spheroid meatball of North European origin, eaten hot or cold, and typically consisting of: minced pork or beef/veal (or, commonly, a blend of these meats); chopped onions; eggs; milk or wat
