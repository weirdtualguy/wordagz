# Runyakitara

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A standardized language comprised of Nyoro (Runyoro), Kiga (also Chiga or Rukiga), Nkore (Runyankole), and Tooro (Rutooro), mostly spoken in Uganda.
