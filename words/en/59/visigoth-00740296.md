# Visigoth

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any member of an ancient East Germanic tribe, one branch of the Goths (the Ostrogoths being the other), which participated in several wars with Rome and established a kingdom with Toulouse for its cap
