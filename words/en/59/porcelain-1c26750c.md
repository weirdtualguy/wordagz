# porcelain

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A hard white translucent ceramic, originally made by firing kaolin, quartz, and feldspar at high temperatures but now also inclusive of similar artificial materials; also often (figurative) such a mat
