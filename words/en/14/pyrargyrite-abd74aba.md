# pyrargyrite

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A sulfosalt mineral used as a silver ore; it is dark red or black in color with a metallic adamantine luster, a sulfide of antimony and silver, Ag₃SbS₃, and occurs in rhombohedral crystals.
