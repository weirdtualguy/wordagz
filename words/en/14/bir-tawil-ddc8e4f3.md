# Bir Tawil

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A region of Africa, along the border between Egypt and Sudan, currently considered as terra nullius. It is declared by Egypt to be Sudanese and Sudan to be Egyptian, and claimed by neither country.
