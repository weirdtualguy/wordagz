# Rāma

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A mythological king of Ayodhya in ancient India, considered to be the seventh avatar of Vishnu and a lila-avatara described in Bhagavata Purana; one of the most worshipped Hindu deities.
