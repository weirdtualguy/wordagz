# toluene

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A colourless, inflammable liquid hydrocarbon, methylbenzene, CH₃.C₆H₅, used as a solvent, in high-octane fuels and in the production of many chemical compounds.
