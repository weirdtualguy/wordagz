# psilocybin

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A hallucinogenic alkaloid, C₁₂H₁₅N₂O·H₂PO₃, present in several species of Central American mushroom and producing effects similar to LSD. The prodrug of psilocin.
