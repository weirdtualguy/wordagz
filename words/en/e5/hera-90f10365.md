# Hera

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The queen of the gods, and goddess of marriage and birth; daughter of Cronus and Rhea, sister and wife of Zeus, mother of Hephaestus, Ares, Hebe, and Enyo.
