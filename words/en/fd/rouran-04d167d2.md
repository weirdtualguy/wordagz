# Rouran

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A khaganate that ruled much of present-day Mongolia, northern China and southern Siberia from the mid-4th to the mid-6th centuries, formed from a tribal confederation of pre-Mongolic peoples of Donghu
