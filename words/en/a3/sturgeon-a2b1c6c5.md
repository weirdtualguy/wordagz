# sturgeon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of the marine or freshwater fish of the family Acipenseridae that are prized for their roe and are endemic to temperate seas and rivers of the northern hemisphere, especially central Eurasia.
