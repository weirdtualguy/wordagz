# ta'ziyeh

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A dramatic art form of Iran performed by Shi'ites and reenacting the martyrdom of Husayn ibn Ali at the Battle of Karbala, usually performed in the month of Muharram.
