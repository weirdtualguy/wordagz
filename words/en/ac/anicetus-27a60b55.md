# Anicetus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

One of the sons of Heracles and Hebe, his brother is Alexiares. Along with their father, Anicetus and Alexiares are the guardians and gatekeepers of the gates of Mount Olympus.
