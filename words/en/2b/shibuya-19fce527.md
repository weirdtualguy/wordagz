# Shibuya

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A special ward of Tokyo prefecture, Japan that is surrounded by (clockwise from north) Shinjuku, Minato, Shinagawa, Meguro, Setagaya, Suginami, and Nakano special wards.
