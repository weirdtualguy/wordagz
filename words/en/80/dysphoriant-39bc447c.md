# dysphoriant

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An agent which can create a state of feeling unwell or unhappy; a stimulus of feeling(s) of emotional and mental discomfort and suffering from restlessness, malaise, depression or anxiety. See dysphor
