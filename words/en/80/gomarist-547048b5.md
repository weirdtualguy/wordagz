# Gomarist

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A follower of Franciscus Gomarus, a 17th-century Dutch Calvinist who strongly opposed the Arminians; an adherent of orthodox Reformed soteriology in the Arminian controversy.
