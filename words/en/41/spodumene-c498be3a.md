# spodumene

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The mineral lithium aluminium inosilicate (LiAl(SiO₃)₂), a pyroxene and ore of lithium, sometimes regarded as a gemstone, which may be greenish, yellowish or pinkish in appearance.
