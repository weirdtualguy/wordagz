# nigorobuna

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A freshwater ray-finned fish (Carassius auratus grandoculis), a wild subspecies of goldfish endemic to Japan, its habitat limited to Lake Biwa and environs.
