# Izanami

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A Japanese creator goddess who is also (along with the Shinigami and Enma) a goddess of death and the underworld; she is Izanagi's younger sister and spouse, and the mother of Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi, Su
