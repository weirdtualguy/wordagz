# harpsichord

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A stringed musical instrument with a keyboard, the mechanical precusor to the fortepiano, in which each key causes a plectrum to pluck a corresponding tuned string, producing a bright, sharp tone simi
