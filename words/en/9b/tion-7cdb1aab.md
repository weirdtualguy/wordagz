# -tion

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** suffix

## Definition

Used to form nouns meaning "the action of (a verb)" or "the result of (a verb)". Words ending in this suffix are almost always derived from a similar Latin word; a few (e.g. gumption) are not derived
