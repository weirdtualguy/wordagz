# glossolalia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Speaking a language one does not know, or speaking elaborate but apparently meaningless speech, while in a trance-like state (or, supposedly, under the influence of a deity or spirits); speaking in to
