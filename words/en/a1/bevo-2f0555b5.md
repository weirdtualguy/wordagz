# BeVo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A style of fabric weave originally produced by punchcard Jacquard looms from the companies of Lucas Vorsteher and Ewald Vorsteher in Wuppertal-Barmen (later merging to become the Beteiligung Vorsteher
