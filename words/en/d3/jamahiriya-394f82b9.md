# Jamahiriya

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

State of the masses; people's republic; the form of the Libyan state ruled by Muammar Gaddafi from 1977-2011, during which time it was known first as the Socialist People's Libyan Arab Jamahiriya then
