# Ameretat

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Zoroastrian divinity/divine concept of "not dying". As the hypostasis of immortality, Ameretat is the Amesha Spenta of long life on earth and eternal life in the hereafter.
