# Satyvati

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The queen of the Kuru king Shantanu, mother of Vyasa, Chitrangada, and Vichitravirya, and grandmother of Dhritarashtra and Pandu in the Mahabharata.
