# coolie

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An unskilled Asian worker, usually of Chinese or Indian descent; a labourer; a porter. Coolies were frequently transported to other countries in the 19th and early 20th centuries as indentured laboure
