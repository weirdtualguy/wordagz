# narwhal

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An Arctic toothed whale (Monodon monoceros) that grows to about 20 feet (6 meters) long, the male having a single horn-like tusk, a twisted, pointed cuspid that projects forward.
