# kugel fountain

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A water feature or sculpture having a sphere that sits in a fitted hollow in a pedestal, supported by aquaplaning on a thin film of water, so that despite its weight it can be turned by hand.
