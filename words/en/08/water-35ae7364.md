# water

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An inorganic compound (of molecular formula H₂O) found at room temperature and pressure as a clear liquid; it is present naturally as rain, and found in rivers, lakes and seas; its solid form is ice a
