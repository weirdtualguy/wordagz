# lignum rhodium

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The fragrant wood of several shrubs and trees, especially of species Convolvulus scoparius and Convolvulus floridus (Convolvulaceae) from the Canary Islands, and of the West Indian Amyris balsamifera
