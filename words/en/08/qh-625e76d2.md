# qh

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adv

## Definition

quaque hora: every hour - used in medical pharmacy prescriptions usually with a numeral defining hours between doses. "q4h" read as: "taken every 4 hours". Also common is the format: "3 caps q4h" read
