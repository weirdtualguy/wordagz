# Hungarism

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The fascist ideology of the Nazi-aligned Arrow Cross Party, which ruled Hungary from 1944 to 1945, and of its predecessors and subsequent far-right supporters.
