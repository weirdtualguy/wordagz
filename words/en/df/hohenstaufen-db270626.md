# Hohenstaufen

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A mediaeval (11th—14th centuries) German dynasty of Swabian origin, from which came numerous monarchs, including dukes of Swabia and emperors of the Holy Roman Empire.
