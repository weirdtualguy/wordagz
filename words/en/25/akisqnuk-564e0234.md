# ʔakisq̓nuk

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Ktunaxa First Nation based in the Kootenays district of British Columbia in Canada, in the British Columbia Treaty Process and part of the Ktunaxa Nation.
