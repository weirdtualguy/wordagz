# Kyuquot

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Indigenous people to Vancouver Island, part of the Nuu-chah-nulth and the Ḵa:'yu:'k't'h'-Che:ḵ'tles7et'h' First Nation (Kyuquot/Cheklesahht First Nation).
