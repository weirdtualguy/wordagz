# Enma

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Japanese name for Yama, the Hindu, Buddhist and Chinese god who presides over naraku (hell). A deity and/or personification of death. (Compare Izanami and the Shinigami.)
