# grootslang

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A South African cryptid with the head of an elephant and the body of a snake, which reputedly eats elephants and hoards diamonds; one particular one (the Grootslang) is reputed to live in a cave in So
