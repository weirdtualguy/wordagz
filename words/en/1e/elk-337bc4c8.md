# elk

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of the subspecies of the moose (Alces alces, the largest member of the deer family, alternatively named Eurasian elk to avoid confusion with the wapiti), that occurs only in Europe and Asia.
