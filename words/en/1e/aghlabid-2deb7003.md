# Aghlabid

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any member of an Arab dynasty of emirs who ruled Ifriqiya and parts of Southern Italy, nominally on behalf of the Abbasid Caliph, from 800–909 CE.
