# nitidine

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A type of benzophenanthridine alkaloid that derives from various species of Zanthoxylum, but most notably Zanthoxylum nitidum.

## Direct ancestors

- [Zanthoxylum nitidum](../../mul/08/zanthoxylum-nitidum-01c1f9ba.md) — Translingual *(derived)*
