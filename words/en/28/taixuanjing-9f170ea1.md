# Taixuanjing

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A Chinese divinatory text composed by the Confucian writer Yang Xiong (53 BCE-18 CE), similar to and inspired by the I Ching, and having 81 ternary tetragrams.
