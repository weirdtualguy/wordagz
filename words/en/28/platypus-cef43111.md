# platypus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A semiaquatic monotreme from eastern Australia with a bill resembling that of a duck, that has a mole-like body, a tail resembling that of a beaver, a waterproof pelt, and flat webbed feet (Ornithorhy
