# alkali

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

One of a class of caustic bases, such as soda, soda ash, caustic soda, potash, ammonia, and lithia, whose distinguishing characteristics are dissolving in alcohol and water, uniting with oils and fats
