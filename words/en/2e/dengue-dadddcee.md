# dengue

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An acute febrile disease of the (sub)tropics caused by the Dengue virus, a flavivirus, transmitted by Aedes mosquitoes, and characterized by high fever, rash, headache, and severe muscle and joint pai
