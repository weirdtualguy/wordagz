# flugelhorn

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A brass instrument resembling a cornet but with a wider, conical bore, and usually with three valves, in the same B-flat pitch as many trumpets and cornets but with a more deeply conical mouthpiece th
