# Charles' Wain

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A bright circumpolar asterism of the northern sky, said to resemble a wagon or cart. It is part of the constellation Ursa Major and includes the seven stars Dubhe, Merak, Phecda, Megrez, Alioth, Mizar
