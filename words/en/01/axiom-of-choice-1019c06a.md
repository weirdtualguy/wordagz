# axiom of choice

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

One of the axioms of set theory, equivalent to the statement that an arbitrary direct product of non-empty sets is non-empty; any version of said axiom, for example specifying the cardinality of the n
