# solenopsin

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An alkaloid, with chemical formula C₁₇H₃₅N, that is considered the primary toxin in fire ant venom and that inhibits angiogenesis via the phosphoinositol-3 kinase signaling pathway.
