# itaconic acid

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

One of the isomeric dicarboxylic acids produced by the distillation of citric acid, or as metabolites by microorganisms, CH₂=C(CO₂H)-CH₂CO₂H.
