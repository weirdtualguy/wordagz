# Abrasax

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A theonym of unclear signification, commonly invoked as a magic word on amulets, talismans, and papyri in the Mediterranean basin from the 2nd century B.C. until the 13th century, and viewed in Gnosti
