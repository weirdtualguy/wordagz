# Pherecrates

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A celebrated Athenian poet of the Old Comedy, living in the fifth century BCE (born after Crates and Cratinus but before Aristophanes, Eupolis, and Plato Comicus – so between c. 519 and c. 446 BCE), v
