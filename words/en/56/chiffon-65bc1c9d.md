# chiffon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A lightweight, balanced plain-woven sheer fabric, or gauze, like gossamer, woven of alternate S- and Z-twist crepe (high-twist) yarns made of silk, polyester, or cotton.
