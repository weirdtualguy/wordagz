# Ivanivtsi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former khutir in Rodatychi silrada, Horodok Raion, Lviv Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, annexed to the silrada’s administrative centre, Rodatychi, somewhen 1947–1967.
