# granite

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A group of igneous and plutonic rocks composed primarily of feldspar and quartz. Usually contains one or more dark minerals, which may be mica, pyroxene, or amphibole. Granite is quarried for building
