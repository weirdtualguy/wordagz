# silog

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A class of Filipino breakfast dishes containing garlic fried rice and fried egg, sunny side up, served with various accompanying savory dishes, usually fried meat dishes such as tapa, longganisa or ha
