# gusle

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A single-stringed lute-like musical instrument with a bowl-shaped body, held vertically in the lap and played with a bow, originating among the Slavic peoples in the Balkans, especially in the Dinarid
