# Bunkyō

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A special ward of Tokyo prefecture, Japan that is surrounded by (clockwise from north) Kita, Arakawa, Taitō, Chiyoda, Shinjuku, and Toshima special wards.
