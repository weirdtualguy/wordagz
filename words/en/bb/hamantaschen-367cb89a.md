# hamantaschen

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Traditional Ashkenazi three-cornered cookies eaten during the Jewish holiday of Purim. The filling may be made from variously from poppy seeds, prunes, nuts, dates, apricots, fruit preserves, chocolat
