# Arlberg Pass

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A mountain pass in Vorarlberg and Tyrol, Austria crossing the Arlberg massif, connecting the state of Vorarlberg and the Klostertal in the west with the state of Tyrol and the Stanzer Tal in the east;
