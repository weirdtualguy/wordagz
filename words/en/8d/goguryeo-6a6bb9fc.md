# Goguryeo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient kingdom in modern Korea, one of the Three Kingdoms, existing from 37 BCE to 668 CE; at its maximum covering all of North Korea, the northern part of South Korea, parts of Manchuria in north
