# Phaophi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The second month of the later ancient Egyptian civil calendar and Coptic calendar, corresponding to the second month of the season of Akhet. Since 25 BCE, when the calendar was reformed to include lea
