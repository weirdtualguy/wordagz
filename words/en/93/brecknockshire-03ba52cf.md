# Brecknockshire

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An inland traditional county of Wales, bordered by Monmouthshire, Herefordshire, Radnorshire, Cardiganshire, Carmarthenshire and Glamorganshire.
