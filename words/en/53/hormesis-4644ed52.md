# hormesis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A phenomenon in which an environmental agent or stressor produces a stimulatory or beneficial effect at low doses and an inhibitory or harmful effect at higher doses, corresponding to a biphasic dose-
