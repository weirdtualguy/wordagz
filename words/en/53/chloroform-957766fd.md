# chloroform

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A halogenated hydrocarbon, trichloromethane, CHCl₃; it is a volatile, sweet-smelling liquid, used extensively as a solvent and formerly as an anesthetic.
