# bidgee-widgee

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A small prostrate, rosaceous, perennial plant, Acaena novae-zelandiae, native to New Zealand, Australia and New Guinea; now an invasive weed in Britain and western Europe.
