# phoenix

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A mythological bird, said to be the only one of its kind, which lives for 500 years and then dies by burning to ashes on a pyre of its own making, ignited by the sun. It then arises anew from the ashe
