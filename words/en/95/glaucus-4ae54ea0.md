# Glaucus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A Greek prophetic sea god, born mortal and turned immortal upon eating a magical herb. It was believed that he commonly came to the rescue of sailors and fishermen in storms, having once been one hims
