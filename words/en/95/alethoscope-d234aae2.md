# alethoscope

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An instrument, invented in the 1860s, for viewing magnified pictures by means of a single lens inside a box. By varying the intensity of the light, the same picture can be shown as "daytime" or "night
