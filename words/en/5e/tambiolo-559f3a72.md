# tambiolo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A fish bowl or barrel-shaped container where balls used in a raffle or a lottery are placed. In the case of a raffle, tickets may also be used. Can be made out of wire, glass, clear plastic, etc. Ofte
