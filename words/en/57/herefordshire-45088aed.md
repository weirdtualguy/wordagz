# Herefordshire

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A ceremonial county and unitary authority in the West Midlands of England, bordered by Shropshire, Worcestershire, Gloucestershire, Monmouthshire, Radnorshire and Brecknockshire.
