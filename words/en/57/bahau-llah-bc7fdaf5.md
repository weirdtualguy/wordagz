# Baháʼu'lláh

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A title given to Mírzá Ḥusayn-ʻAlí Núrí (1817–1892), Persian religious leader, prophet-founder of the Baháʼí Faith.
