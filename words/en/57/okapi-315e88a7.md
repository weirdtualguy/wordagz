# okapi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A ruminant (Okapia johnstoni) found in the rainforests of the Congo, related to the giraffe but with a much shorter neck, a reddish-brown coat, and zebra-like stripes on the hindquarters.
