# vimpa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A veil or shawl worn over the shoulders of servers who carry the mitre and crosier during liturgical functions when they are not being used by the bishop, in the Roman Catholic, Anglican, and some oth
