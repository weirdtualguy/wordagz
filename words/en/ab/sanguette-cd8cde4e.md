# sanguette

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A blood dish from rural Occitania made immediately upon exsanguination of livestock by collecting, preparing, and cooking the fresh blood; typically a crepe or pancake but varies by town across Occita
