# Aleutian Islands

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An archipelago of more than three hundred small volcanic islands forming an island arc in the Northern Pacific Ocean, between Alaska and the Kamchatka Peninsula of Asia; part of the United States.
