# momo

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A type of dumpling, typical of the cuisines of Nepal, Ladakh, and Tibet and also regionally popular elsewhere in Asia, made with a simple flour and water dough and filled with meat, vegetables, or che
