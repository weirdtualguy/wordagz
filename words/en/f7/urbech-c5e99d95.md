# urbech

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A thick, sticky paste made from ground raw or dried seeds (such as flax, hemp, sunflower, pumpkin), apricot kernels or nuts, traditionally used in Dagestani cuisine.
