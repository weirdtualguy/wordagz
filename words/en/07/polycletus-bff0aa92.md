# Polycletus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Alternative form of Polyclitus, A male given name from Latin Polyclētus \[in turn from Ancient Greek Πολύκλειτος (Polúkleitos, “far-famed, greatly renowned”)\].
