# Miao

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The Miao peoples (including Hmong, Hmu, A-Hmao, Qo Xiong, etc.), an ethnic group of China and Vietnam speaking the Hmong-Mien language family.
