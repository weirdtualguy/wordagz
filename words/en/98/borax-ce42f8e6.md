# borax

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A white or gray/grey crystalline salt, found in nature as a mineral, with a slight alkaline taste, used as a flux, in soldering metals, making enamels, fixing colors/colours on porcelain, and as a soa
