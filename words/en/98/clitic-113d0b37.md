# clitic

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A morpheme that functions like a word, but never appears as an independent word, instead being always attached to a following or preceding word (or, in some cases, within a surrounding word).

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [κλιτικός](../../grc/c9/w-a8f9ec61.md) ("inflexional").
