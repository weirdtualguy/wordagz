# chancel

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The space around the altar in a church or cathedral, often enclosed, for use by the clergy and the choir. In medieval cathedrals the chancel was usually enclosed or blocked off from the nave by an alt
