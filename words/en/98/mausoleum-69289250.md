# Mausoleum

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The grand tomb built around 353–351 BCE at Halicarnassus for Mausolus, a ruler of Caria and satrap of the Achaemenid Empire; one of the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World.
