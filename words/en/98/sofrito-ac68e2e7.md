# sofrito

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A sautéed mixture of chopped onions, garlic, and tomato and/or (chili) peppers, and sometimes carrots, celery, and/or herbs, used as the beginning point of a dish, especially in Hispanic or Portuguese
