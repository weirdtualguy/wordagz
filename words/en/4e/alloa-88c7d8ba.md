# Alloa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A town which is the administrative centre of Clackmannanshire council area, Scotland, and was the county town of the historic county of Clackmannanshire between 1822 and 1975 (OS grid ref NS8893).
