# Kisshōten

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Japanese version of the Hindu goddess Lakshmi: goddess of happiness, charm and beauty, of fertility, nature, and of human creativity, and the patronesses of joy, amusement and festivities.
