# kokoda

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Fijian dish of raw fish marinated in citrus juice (especially lime or lemon), typically mixed with coconut cream, onions, chillies, and tomatoes; the Fijian version of ceviche.
