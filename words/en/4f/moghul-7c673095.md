# Moghul

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A head of the Timurid dynasty founded by Zahīr-ud-Dīn Muhammad Bābur (1483-1530) which controlled large parts of southern Asia from the 16th to the 19th centuries.
