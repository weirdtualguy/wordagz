# Yenish

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A group of nomadic people in Europe, living mostly in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, Wallonia, and parts of France. They often claim to be descendants of the Celtic Helvetii.
