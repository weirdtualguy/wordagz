# Chloris

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A nymph and the goddess of flowers and spring. She is a daughter of Oceanus and Tethys, the wife of Zephyrus, and mother of Karpos. Her Roman counterpart is Flora.
