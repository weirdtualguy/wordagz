# Heraclitus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A transliteration of the Ancient Greek male given name Ἡράκλειτος (Hērákleitos), notably borne by Heraclitus of Ephesus, a pre-Socratic Ionian philosopher.
