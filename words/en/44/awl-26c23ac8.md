# awl

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A pointed instrument for piercing small holes, as in leather or wood; used by shoemakers, saddlers, cabinetmakers, etc. The blade is differently shaped and pointed for different uses, as in the brad a
