# Tabriz

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A city in Iran, the seat of Tabriz County's Central District and the capital of East Azerbaijan Province. Fourth largest city of Iran with population of about 1,600,000. Historical names: Davrezh, Tav
