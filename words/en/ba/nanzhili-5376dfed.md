# Nanzhili

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former Chinese province of the Ming Dynasty which contained the capital of Nanjing, later renamed Jiangnan by the Qing Dynasty and divided into Jiangsu and Anhui under the Qianlong Emperor.
