# Holy Spirit

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The person (hypostasis) of the Trinity or Godhead corresponding to divine essence present in the faithful (particularly inspired prophets) and considered to proceed either from (Eastern Orthodoxy) God
