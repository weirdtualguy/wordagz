# Bohai Sea

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A gulf of the Yellow Sea in the northern Pacific Ocean beside Shandong, Hebei, Tianjin, and Liaoning on the northeastern coast of mainland China.
