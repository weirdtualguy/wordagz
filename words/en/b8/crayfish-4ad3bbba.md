# crayfish

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of numerous freshwater decapod crustaceans in superfamily Astacoidea or Parastacoidea, resembling the related lobster but usually much smaller.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [\*krabit](../../frk/86/krabit-6bc906db.md) ("crab").
