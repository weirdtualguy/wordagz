# crépinette

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A small, round, flattened sausage, shaped like a patty, made from minced or ground meat or even from truffles, and wrapped in caul fat. They are usually cooked with an outer coating of bread and sauté
