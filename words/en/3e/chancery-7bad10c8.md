# chancery

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

In England, formerly, the highest court of judicature next to the Parliament, exercising jurisdiction at law, but chiefly in equity; but under the jurisdiction act of 1873 it became the chancery divis
