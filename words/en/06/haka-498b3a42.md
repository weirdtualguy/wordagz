# haka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A group dance of New Zealand's Māori people featuring rhythmic chanting, vigorous facial and arm movements, and foot stamping. Traditionally a war dance, today it is also performed to welcome guests,
