# instant mami

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An instant noodle soup dish that is popular in the Philippines and abroad, consisting of pre-cooked noodles with a packet of soup, seasoning, and oil that can be prepared by adding hot water.
