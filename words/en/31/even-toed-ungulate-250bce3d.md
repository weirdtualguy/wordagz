# even-toed ungulate

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any mammal in the order Artiodactyla, including camels, pigs and ruminants such as deer, cattle, goats, sheep and antelopes; as well as, in recent years, cetaceans (whales, dolphins, and porpoises).
