# Papiaments

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A dialect-neutral spelling of Papiamento or Papiamentu, a creole language spoken in the ABC Islands (the former spelling is used in Aruba while the latter is used in Bonaire and Curaçao)
