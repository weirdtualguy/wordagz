# Erebuni

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

a fortification and city of the ancient kingdom of Urartu, located on the hill Arin Berd in modern-day Yerevan, capital of Armenia; founded in 782 BC by king Argishti I
