# limma

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of several small musical intervals, such as the semitone. Most commonly referrs to the diatonic semitone or minor second, which has a ratio of approximately 16/15 or 27/25 in meantone temperaments
