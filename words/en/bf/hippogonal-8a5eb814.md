# hippogonal

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Being a move in a board game that is neither orthogonal nor diagonal, as a knight's move in chess. In chess, an (m,n) move spans m squares in one of the orthogonal directions and n squares in a perpen
