# sublimation

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The transition of a substance from the solid phase directly to the vapor state such that it does not pass through the intermediate liquid phase.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [sublīmō](../../la/8c/sublimo-ad8e8691.md) ("to raise, to elevate").
