# Hromokliia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A river, a right tributary of the Inhul, having its course entirely within the Kropyvnytskyi Raion of Kirovohrad Oblast and the Bashtanka Raion of Mykolaiv Oblast of Ukraine.
