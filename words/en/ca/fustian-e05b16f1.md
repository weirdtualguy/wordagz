# fustian

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Originally, a kind of coarse fabric made from cotton and flax; now, a kind of coarse twilled cotton, or cotton and linen, stuff with a short pile and often dyed a dull colour, which is chiefly prepare
