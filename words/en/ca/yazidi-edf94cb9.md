# Yazidi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A member of an endogamous, largely Kurmanji-speaking ethnoreligious group which practices Yazidism and is natively concentrated mostly in Syria, Iraq, and the Caucasus.
