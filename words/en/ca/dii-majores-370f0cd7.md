# Dii Majores

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The twelve great gods of Roman mythology, namely Juno, Vesta, Minerva, Ceres, Diana, Venus, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Neptune, Vulcan, and Apollo.
