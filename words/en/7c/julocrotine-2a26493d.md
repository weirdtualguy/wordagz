# julocrotine

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A glutarimide alkaloid, N-\[2,6-dioxo-1-(2-phenylethyl)-3-piperidinyl\]-2-methylbutanamide, present in Julocroton montevidensis and Croton membranaceus.
