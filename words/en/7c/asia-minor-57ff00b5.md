# Asia Minor

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A large peninsula, mostly coterminous with Anatolia, between the Mediterranean Sea, the Aegean Sea and the Black Sea; it makes up the Asian part of Turkey.
