# halfa grass

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A north African and south Asian grass of species Desmostachya bipinnata, with tough stems woven into rope, sandals, mats, etc., and sacred in Hinduism, Buddhism and other religions.
