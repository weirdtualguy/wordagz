# baglamas

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A plucked stringed instrument, a long-necked bowl lute, played in Greek music and often made of improvised materials; it is a high-pitched and small bouzouki with one string in an octave pair on the l
