# alkermes

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The desiccated bodies of certain insects (female Kermes ilicis and Kermes vermilio), which live on the kermes oak, formerly much used as a material for dye and a medicinal ingredient; also a given mix
