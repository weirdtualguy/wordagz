# steinstrasse

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A complication of extracorporeal shock wave lithotripsy (ESWL) in the treatment of kidney stones, in which multiple small stone fragments line up within the ureter, causing obstruction.
