# mezuzah

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A piece of parchment inscribed with specific Hebrew verses from the Torah (Deuteronomy 6:4–9 and 11:13–21) and attached in a case to the doorpost of a house.
