# tensor fasciae latae

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A muscle that arises especially from the anterior part of the iliac crest and from the anterior superior iliac spine, is inserted into the iliotibial band of the fascia lata about one third of the way
