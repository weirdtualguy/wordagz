# colocynth

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A viny plant, of species Citrullus colocynthis, native to the Mediterranean Basin and Asia. It produces a lemon-sized, yellowish, green-mottled, spongy, and extremely bitter fruit.
