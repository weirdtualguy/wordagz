# vinegret

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A type of Russian salad with diced cooked vegetables (red beets, potatoes, carrots), chopped onions, as well as sauerkraut or brined pickles or both, and sometimes other ingredients such as green peas
