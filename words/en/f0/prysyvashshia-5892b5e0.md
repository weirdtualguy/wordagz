# Prysyvashshia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A geographic region of southern Ukraine, surrounding the Syvash; it occupies those parts of eastern left-bank Kherson Oblast outside the Dnieper basin and the northeastern coastal strip of the Crimea
