# bedeguar

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A gall produced on rosebushes, especially on the sweetbrier or eglantine, by a puncture from the ovipositor of a gallfly (Diplolepis rosae). It was once supposed to have medicinal properties.
