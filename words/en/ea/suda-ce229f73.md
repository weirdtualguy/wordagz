# Suda

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A comprehensive 10th-century Byzantine encyclopaedia, written in Greek, containing approximately 30,000 entries on historical, biographical, literary, and lexical topics from the ancient Mediterranean
