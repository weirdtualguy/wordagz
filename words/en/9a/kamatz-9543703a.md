# kamatz

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Hebrew nikud vowel sign, shaped like a small uppercase T, which is written beneath a letter to indicate an /a/ sound (or, in Ashkenazi pronunciation, an /o/ sound).
