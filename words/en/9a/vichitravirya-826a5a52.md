# Vichitravirya

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Kuru king of Hastinapura, younger son of Shantanu and Satyavati, husband of Ambika and Ambalika, and the father of Dhritarashtra and Pandu in the Mahabharata.
