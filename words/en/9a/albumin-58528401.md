# albumin

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

Any of a class of monomeric proteins that are soluble in water, and are coagulated by heat; they occur in egg white, milk etc; they function as carrier protein for steroids, fatty acids, and thyroid h
