# epiphenomenon

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An activity, process, or state that is the result of another; a by-product, a consequence.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [φαινόμενον](../../grc/4a/w-87466a57.md) ("thing appearing to view").
