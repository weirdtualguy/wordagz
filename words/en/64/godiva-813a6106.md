# Godiva

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An 11th-century Anglo-Saxon noblewoman who, according to legend, rode unclothed through the streets of Coventry to gain a remission of the oppressive taxation that her husband had imposed on his tenan
