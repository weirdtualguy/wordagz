# Kingdom of the Two Sicilies

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former kingdom in southern Italy with its capital at Palermo and Naples between 1816 and 1861, covering Sicily and most of the present-day Mezzogiorno.
