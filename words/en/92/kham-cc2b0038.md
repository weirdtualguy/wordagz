# Kham

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

One of the three traditional regions of Tibet, covering a land area largely divided between eastern parts of the Tibet Autonomous Region, western parts of Sichuan, and smaller parts of Qinghai and Yun
