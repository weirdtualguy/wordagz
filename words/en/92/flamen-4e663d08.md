# flamen

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A priest devoted to the service of a particular god, from whom he received a distinguishing epithet. The most honored were those of Jupiter, Mars, and Quirinus, called respectively Flamen Dialis, Flam
