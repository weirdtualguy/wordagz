# Starostyne

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Former name of Starosillia: a village, the administrative centre of Chobruchi starostynskyi okruh, Rozdilna urban hromada, Rozdilna Raion, Odesa Oblast, Ukraine; known by this name from February 1945
