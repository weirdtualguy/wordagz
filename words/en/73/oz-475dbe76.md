# Oz

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Ellipsis of Land of Oz, the fictional setting created by L. Frank Baum for The Wonderful Wizard of Oz, and other works. The term has been extended from the original country of Oz to the entire fiction
