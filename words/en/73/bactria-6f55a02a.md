# Bactria

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The ancient name of the country between the range of the Hindu Kush and the Amu Darya in West Asia, encompassing parts of northern Afghanistan, eastern Turkmenistan, southern Uzbekistan, Tajikistan an
