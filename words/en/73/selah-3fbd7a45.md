# selah

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** intj

## Definition

A word occurring between verses or paragraphs in parts of the Hebrew Bible, namely in Habakkuk and the Psalms; perhaps indicating a pause, either for contemplation or for clearing the throat in singin
