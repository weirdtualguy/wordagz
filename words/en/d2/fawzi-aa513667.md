# Fawzi

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A male given name from Arabic, meaning “victorious, triumphant”, feminine equivalent Fawzia, Fauzia, Faouzia, and Fouzia; variant forms Fawzy, Faouzi
