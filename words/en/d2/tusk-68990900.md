# tusk

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

One of a pair of elongated pointed teeth (either cuspids or incisors) that extend outside the mouth of an animal such as walrus, elephant or wild boar, and which continue to grow throughout the animal
