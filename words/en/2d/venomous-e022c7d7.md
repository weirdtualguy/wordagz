# venomous

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of an animal (specifically a snake) or parts of its body: producing venom (“a toxin intended for defensive or offensive use”) which is usually injected into an enemy or prey by biting or stinging; hen
