# declaim

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** verb

## Definition

To recite, e.g., poetry, in a theatrical way; to speak for rhetorical display; to speak pompously, noisily, or theatrically; bemouth; to make an empty speech; to rehearse trite arguments in debate; to
