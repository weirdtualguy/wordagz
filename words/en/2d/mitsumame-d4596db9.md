# mitsumame

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Japanese dessert made of small cubes of agar jelly, served in a bowl with boiled red field peas (or sometimes azuki beans), often gyūhi, shiratama dango and a variety of fruits such as peach slices,
