# Liubashivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former village in Yurkivka silrada, Uman Raion, Cherkasy Oblast, Ukrainian Soviet Socialist Republic, first mentioned in 1775, annexed to the silrada’s administrative centre, Yurkivka, in July 1958.
