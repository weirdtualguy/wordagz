# obelus

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A symbol resembling a horizontal line (–), sometimes together with one or two dots (for example, ⨪ or ÷), which was used in ancient manuscripts and texts to mark a word or passage as doubtful or spuri
