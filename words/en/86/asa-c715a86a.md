# Asa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The third king of Judah mentioned in the Books of Kings who succeeded his father Abijah; the father of King Jehoshaphat. (Matthew calls him "Asaph" in Matthew 1:7.)
