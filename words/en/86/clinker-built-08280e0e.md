# clinker-built

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of a boat or ship: where the hull planks overlap each other edge-to-edge, similar to shingles on a roof, and are secured by clenched nails or rivets for a strong, watertight seal.
