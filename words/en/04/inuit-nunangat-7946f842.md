# Inuit Nunangat

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The collective name of the territories of the Inuit in Canada, including the Inuvialuit Settlement Region, the federal territory of Nunavut, the Nunavik region in northern Quebec, and the Nunatsiavut
