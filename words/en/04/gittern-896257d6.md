# gittern

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A small, quill-plucked, gut-strung musical instrument, most commonly with three to four strings in doubles courses; it is a flat-backed predecessor of the guitar, and it originated around the 13th cen
