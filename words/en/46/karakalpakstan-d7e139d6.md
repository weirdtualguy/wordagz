# Karakalpakstan

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An autonomous republic in northwestern Uzbekistan, bordering Kazakhstan and Turkmenistan. Official name: Republic of Karakalpakstan. Capital: Nukus.
