# Bhaga

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

One of the Adityas, a god of wealth and marriage in Hinduism. In the Rigveda Bhaga is the god who supervises the distribution of goods and destiny to each man corresponding to his merits.
