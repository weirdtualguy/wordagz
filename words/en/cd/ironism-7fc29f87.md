# ironism

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A state of doubt regarding one's own "vocabulary" (set of communicative beliefs) that cannot be removed by making arguments in that vocabulary.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [εἴρων](../../grc/3e/w-7a70647a.md) ("one who says less than they think").
