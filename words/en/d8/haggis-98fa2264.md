# haggis

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A traditional Scottish dish made from minced sheep offal with oatmeal and spices, etc., originally boiled in the stomach of a sheep but now often in an artificial casing, and usually served with neeps
