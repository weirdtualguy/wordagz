# varicella

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An acute, highly contagious pathogenic disease caused by the varicella zoster virus, primarily affecting children and characterized by a widespread eruptive skin rash of itchy blisters;chicken pox
