# naporitan

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A Japanese dish styled on Neapolitan food; spaghetti topped with ketchup or tomato sauce, onion, button mushrooms, green peppers, bacon and Tabasco sauce.
