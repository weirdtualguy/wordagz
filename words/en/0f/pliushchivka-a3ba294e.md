# Pliushchivka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A former silrada of Bashtanka Raion, Mykolaiv Oblast, Ukraine, established ante September 1926, amalgamated into Bashtanka urban hromada in September 2016.
