# Atri

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A legendary bard and scholar, one of nine Prajapatis, a son of Brahma, said to be ancestor of some Brahmins. Atri is one of the Saptarishi (Seven Great Sages) in the seventh, i.e. the present Manvanta
