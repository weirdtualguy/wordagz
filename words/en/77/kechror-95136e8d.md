# Kechror

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A town in medieval Armenia in the Gabeghyank canton of the Ayrarat province of Greater Armenia. Its exact location is unknown. Most likely it was located at the site of (or near) the later town of Kec
