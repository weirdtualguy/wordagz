# mytacism

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A speech disorder manifesting as an abnormal repetition of the sound /m/ or replacement of consonants with m-like sounds.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [μυτακισμός](../../grc/f3/w-b941bdfe.md) ("fondness of the letter mu").
