# Amarone

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An Italian full-bodied ruby to garnet red wine, characterized by a pungent, almost bitter taste. It is made in the Valpolicella region from dried-after-picking grapes of the Corvina, Rondinella and Mo
