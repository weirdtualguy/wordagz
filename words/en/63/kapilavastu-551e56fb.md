# Kapilavastu

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Kapilavastu, a city of ancient India, capital of the Shakyas in late Iron Age India, around the 6th and 5th centuries BC. Early residence of Gautama Buddha and that of his parents Suddhodana and Maya.
