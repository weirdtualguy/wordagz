# mooruk

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

The dwarf cassowary (Casuarius bennetti) found in New Britain, New Guinea, and Yapen Island. It is noted for its agility. It is smaller and has stouter legs than the common cassowary.
