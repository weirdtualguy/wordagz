# -se

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** suffix

## Definition

Forms verbs from adjectives or nouns. When attached to certain adjectives, it forms a transitive verb whose meaning is, to make (adjective). The same construction could also be done to certain (fewer)
