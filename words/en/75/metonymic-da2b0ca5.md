# metonymic

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** adj

## Definition

Of, or relating to, a word or phrase that names an object from a single characteristic of it or of a closely related object.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [μετωνυμικός](../../grc/5d/w-b192be84.md) ("of or like metonymy").
