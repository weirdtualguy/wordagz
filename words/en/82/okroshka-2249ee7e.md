# okroshka

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A cold raw vegetable soup, part of the national cuisines of Russia and Ukraine. Usually made by pouring kvass or kefir in a pot of diced cucumber, radish, green onion, boiled potato, boiled egg, sausa
