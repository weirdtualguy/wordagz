# philosopher's stone

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A supposed substance able to turn base metals, such as lead or mercury, into gold or silver, also sometimes claimed to cure any illness (as panacea) or confer immortality (as elixir of life), among ot
