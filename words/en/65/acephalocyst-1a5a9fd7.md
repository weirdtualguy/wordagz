# acephalocyst

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A larval entozoön in the form of a subglobular or oval vesicle, or hydatid, filled with fluid, sometimes found in the tissues of man and the lower animals; -- so called from the absence of a head or v
