# leeuwendaalder

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A silver coin minted from 1575 by the rebelling Dutch provinces and subsequently the Dutch Republic with a nominal value varying between 32 and 42 stivers, also used for trade in the American colonies
