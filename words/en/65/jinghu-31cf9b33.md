# jinghu

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A two-stringed bamboo musical instrument played with a bow, the smallest and highest-pitched in the huqin family of traditional Chinese instruments, associated mainly with the tradition of Beijing ope
