# bishop

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

An overseer of congregations: either any such overseer, generally speaking, or (in Eastern Orthodoxy, Catholicism, Anglicanism, etc.) an official in the church hierarchy (actively or nominally) govern
