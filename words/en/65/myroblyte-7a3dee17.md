# myroblyte

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A saint whose relics or place of burial are said to have produced the Oil of Saints ("an aromatic liquid with healing properties" or "holy water (very much like myrrh)") or the odour of sanctity.
