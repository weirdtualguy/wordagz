# Serpens

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

A large summer constellation of the northern sky said to resemble a snake. It is the only constellation consisting of two parts (Serpens Caput and Serpens Cauda) separated by the constellation Ophiuch
