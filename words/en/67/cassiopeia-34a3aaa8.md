# Cassiopeia

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

Proud wife of Cepheus and mother of Andromeda, queen of Ethiopia. When she boasted that her beauty was equal to that of the Nereids, she was punished by Poseidon.
