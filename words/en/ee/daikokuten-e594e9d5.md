# Daikokuten

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

The Japanese name for Mahākāla, a Buddhist god of wealth, commerce and trade. In Japanese folk tradition, he is one of the seven gods of luck. In Shinto, he is syncretized with the deity Ōkuninushi. D
