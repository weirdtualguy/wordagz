# Susa

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient city, which became the capital of Elam, the Persian Empire, the Seleucid Empire and the Parthian Empire, now known as Shush in modern-day Khuzestan Province, Iran.
