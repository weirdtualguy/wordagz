# Anaxagoras

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** name

## Definition

An ancient Greek philosopher (c. 500 BCE – 428 BCE) from Clazomenae, who is famous for introducing the cosmological concept of nous (mind), as an ordering force.
