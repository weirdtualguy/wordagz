# -orama

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** suffix

## Definition

Used to form, from one noun, a second meaning "wide view of" the first, or (with ironic reference to the preceding sense) "surfeit of", "overattention to", or "exaggerated praise of" the first.

!> **Semantic drift warning:** this definition shares no meaningful keywords with its root ancestor [ὅρᾱμᾰ](../../grc/a7/w-0de931d8.md) ("sight, spectacle").
