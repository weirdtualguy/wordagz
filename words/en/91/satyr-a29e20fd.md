# satyr

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A sylvan deity or demigod, male companion of Pan or Dionysus, represented as part man and part goat, and characterized by riotous merriment and lasciviousness, sometimes pictured with a perpetual erec
