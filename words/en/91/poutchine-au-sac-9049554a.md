# poutchine au sac

**Language:** English (`en`) · **Part of speech:** noun

## Definition

A kind of pudding in a bag; a mixture of beef suet, flour, brown sugar, raisins, currants, and milk. The ingredients are combined in a cotton bag or sealer jars, then steamed, and topped with a sauce.
